## 8. How to Evaluate a Black Friday Deal

A large percentage discount does not automatically make something a good purchase.

Before buying an online business tool, consider the following.

### Compare the Regular Price

Look at the normal price and compare it with the promotional price.

### Check the Renewal Price

Some services offer a large first year discount but charge substantially more at renewal.

### Check the Subscription Period

A low monthly price may require an annual or multi year commitment.

### Review the Features

Make sure the discounted plan includes the features you actually need.

### Check the Refund Policy

Understand the cancellation and refund conditions before purchasing.

### Consider Long Term Value

Ask yourself:

**Will this tool still be useful to my business six or twelve months from now?**

If you would not buy the product without the discount, the discount may not be enough reason to purchase it.
